# Analysis modes and formulas

Fast: video metadata, transcript, representative frames, existing structured semantic analysis and normalized persistence. Standard adds FFmpeg scene detection, shot metrics, midpoint-per-shot color, silence detection and integrated loudness; visual material classification uses optional CLIP, and audio labels use optional PANNs. Deep additionally connects WhisperX word timing (only when no supplied transcript), optional TransNetV2, Demucs and librosa beats via explicit local model configuration. These connections have not been tested with real model weights. Heavy packages/weights are never auto-installed. Unsupported advanced functions are marked not_available.

Editing: N shots, N-1 internal cuts; average/median/population variance of shot seconds; cuts/min = (N-1)*60000/duration_ms. Pacing bins are 30 seconds with actual last-bin duration.
Color: one 64x64 RGB midpoint sample per shot, HSV brightness/saturation means, saturation-weighted circular hue (null for achromatic shots), dominant quantized RGB bins (32 levels/channel), warm/cool = saturation-weighted cos(hue-30 degrees). Adjacent color change = Euclidean distance between mean linear sRGB vectors / sqrt(3); consistency = 1 - mean adjacent change. Overall saturation/brightness weighted by shot duration. These are transparent engineering proxies, not perceptually calibrated judgments.
Audio: silence below -35 dB for >=0.3 seconds; silence ratio is union duration/video duration. Loudness is FFmpeg EBU R128 integrated LUFS; variation is loudness range (LRA, LU), explicitly not standard deviation. Nonfinite loudness (e.g. digital silence) is null. Speech/music/SFX may overlap; their ratios need not sum to one.
Beat alignment: cuts inside known music intervals matching a music-interval beat within configurable ±150 ms divided by all music-interval cuts. Coverage: music-interval beats matching one of those cuts divided by all music-interval beats. Empty denominators are not_applicable. No classified music intervals means unknown, not all-video music.
Performance: cumulative views / exact elapsed days since publication, not recent daily growth. likes/views and comments/views require positive views. Relative performance = target views / median views of preceding 10 same-channel videos; choose predecessors before dropping missing counts and never substitute older videos. Store candidate IDs, count, denominator and timestamps. Missing/zero median is not a valid denominator; fewer than 10 yields a warning. Present-day snapshots do not equal same-age views.
